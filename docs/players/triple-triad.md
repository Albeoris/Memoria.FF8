# Triple Triad

Config → Triple Triad stores board dimensions, hand size, Random redraws and assistance with the current game. At Title these settings apply to new games. Settings cannot change during a match.

Boards range from 2×2 to 5×5, with width and height independently editable. The default is 3×3 with five cards per hand. Each hand needs at least half the number of cells, rounded up; increasing the board raises the hand size when needed. You can deal up to 50 cards per hand, including more cards than fit on the board. Large hands use multiple columns. Unplayed cards still count toward their owner's score and participate in the normal trade rules.

Hardcore uses at least 4×4 and eight cards per hand. Larger boards and hands remain available. Hardcore permits at most one Random redraw and only Off or Direct assistance.

## Random redraws

The default allowance is zero. Configure up to three redraws and use the Redraw button or Page Up / Page Down before the first move. Only your hand changes; the opponent's cards, elemental cells and starting player stay fixed. Redraws are saved with the current match. If the opponent starts, confirm Begin match after deciding whether to redraw.

## Assistance

- **Off:** the normal presentation.
- **Direct:** a translucent card previews the selected empty cell. Blue arrows identify ordinary captures using elemental adjustments. Predicted enemy cards slowly pulse toward blue.
- **Rules:** also includes Same and Plus captures. Participating numbers shimmer; the explanation shows the printed rank comparisons or matching sums, including Same Wall.
- **Combo:** also displays subsequent capture waves, with the original Combo artwork on their arrows. Combo appears only after a rule trigger that permits it.
- **Hand:** also marks hand cards that can trigger Same or Plus in at least one currently empty cell.

Use the directional keys to choose a cell, or move the pointer over the board after choosing a card. Assistance never changes the match or opponent decisions.

## Elemental numbers

Cards on elemental cells display increased ranks in green and decreased ranks in red, using the effective values used for ordinary captures. A matching element adds one; a mismatch subtracts one. A is ten, so A + 1 displays **B** (eleven), and 1 − 1 displays **0**. Same, Plus and Same Wall continue to use the printed ranks, as in the existing rules.

The scalable parchment board and its ornaments are drawn procedurally. Installed card artwork replacements continue to supply the card faces.
