# GF transformations

[Documentation](../README.md) / Play

Enable **GF Transformations: On** under **Config → Gameplay → Magic and Guardian Forces** or in New Game options. It defaults to Off. During a playthrough the choice belongs to that save and can be changed between battles. Original-game saves load with it Off; enabled saves use Memoria's format.

To transform, choose **Commands → GF → Ifrit → Right → Summon**. Confirming Summon starts the usual GF charge for the selected character. Left or Cancel returns to the same GF selection. Ordinary Confirm directly on Ifrit still performs the complete classic Hell Fire summon. Ifrit must be alive, acquired and junctioned to that character.

After emerging and landing, Ifrit replaces the character's appearance. His own GF level determines his innate commands.

| Ifrit's level | Physical attack | Spells | Finisher |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–14 | Attack | Fire | Hell Fire |
| 15–29 | Heavy Strike | Fire | Hell Fire |
| 30–69 | Heavy Strike | Fira | Hell Fire |
| 70–99 | Heavy Strike | Firaga | Hell Fire |
| 100 | Heavy Strike | Firaga, Flare | Hell Fire |

The character's Strength and Magic drive the physical attacks and spells. Ifrit can cast his innate spells with an empty magic stock. Hell Fire uses its usual GF damage calculation.

Below the innate moves, the menu lists every battle command Ifrit has learned, including those taught with items. Draw, Item, GF and Magic are excluded. A learned command is available even when the character has another command equipped. Its usual targeting, costs and battle restrictions apply. Darkside draws on the character's weapon and spends Ifrit's HP. Ifrit performs the strike with his own animation, which has no gunblade trigger. The menu grows into columns like Draw and the Magic pages. Use Up and Down within a column, Left and Right between columns, or select a command directly with the pointer.

The form uses the elemental and status defenses of Ifrit in the Fire Cavern. It absorbs fire and takes double ice damage. The character's own defenses return with their human form. Damage and healing use Ifrit's HP while transformed, while the character retains its previous HP and statuses. Losing all GF HP returns the character without reviving a genuinely KO'd character.

The form lasts 30 seconds of advancing battle simulation time. Existing battle pauses also pause its lifetime. Hell Fire ends the form early; expiry schedules it after an already committed action completes. If a status prevents the finisher, the character returns immediately. Returning consumes the turn and does not refill either HP pool.

The party can summon one GF at a time. A charge reserves the summon until it is interrupted, the classic summon finishes, or the form returns. Other characters can use their normal commands while the summon is occupied. If Ifrit has learned MiniMog and its usual conditions are met, choosing it returns Ifrit before MiniMog appears.

The form's Hell Fire omits the earlier emergence. Press **Escape battle (left)** (default **D / left trigger**) to skip ahead to the falling meteor finale, which plays at normal speed. This uses the existing remappable battle control. Skip preserves earned Boost and completes the attack and cleanup once. The same input also skips all sixteen ordinary junctionable GF summons to their own finales. The scene holds its last displayed frame while advancing to the finale, and controls remain responsive. Damage, contact counts and status effects still follow the original attack.
